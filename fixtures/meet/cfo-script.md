# The fake CFO in a meeting

A script for the first demo story. Everyone and everything in it is invented.

**Setting.** Laras Pramesti, a finance officer at Ombakura Freight, is called into a video meeting by someone who looks and sounds like her CFO, Budi Prakoso. He is not Budi.

**Who reads what.** One person joins the call under the name Budi Prakoso and reads his lines. The person demonstrating plays Laras and has the side panel open, with captions turned on and set to English.

**What the lines are built to contain.** Authority ("I am the CFO"), urgency ("today, before four o'clock"), secrecy ("keep this between us") and a change of payment details ("the new account").

## Lines

**Budi Prakoso:** Laras, thanks for joining at short notice. I only have a few minutes before I board.

**Laras Pramesti:** Of course. What do you need?

**Budi Prakoso:** We are closing an acquisition this week and it is strictly confidential. Only the board and I know about it.

**Budi Prakoso:** I need you to send a payment of 2.4 billion rupiah to Tessaline Packaging today, before four o'clock.

**Laras Pramesti:** That is far more than their usual invoices. Is it going to the account we have on file?

**Budi Prakoso:** No, they changed banks. Use the new account on the invoice they sent this morning, the one ending 4416.

**Laras Pramesti:** A change of bank details normally has to be confirmed with the supplier first.

**Budi Prakoso:** There is no time for that. I am the CFO and I am authorising it now, so skip the usual checks.

**Budi Prakoso:** And keep this between us. Do not mention it to Bayu or anyone else until the deal is announced.

**Laras Pramesti:** Understood. Let me check one thing before I send it.

## What happens next

Laras opens the side panel, sees the pressure tactics listed, and starts a phone check with the real Budi Prakoso on the number on record. He presses 2: not me.
